    About this preview and its limits

    v0.5 packages the strongest fixed Phase 5 v4 development recipe. Its selection-conditional development estimate is 94.2% accuracy, but the model was not promoted: the Opishnyan/Bubnivka precision boundary missed the frozen gates, scores are uncalibrated, and the sealed test remains untouched.

    Use it for visual exploration and assisted cataloguing—not as an autonomous or official cultural attribution. Public deployment also requires dataset-rights and DINOv3 licence review.

    The five traditions

    A compact field guide.

    Use these hints to compare the model’s alternatives, not as strict rules.

    01

    Opishnyan

    Painted ceramics · compare composition, line rhythm, palette, and vessel form.

    02

    Örnek

    Crimean Tatar symbolic compositions using floral or geometric signs across many crafts.

    UNESCO source
    03

    Bubnivka

    Painted ceramics · compare repeated borders, palette, and motif placement on the vessel.

    04

    Petrykivka

    Decorative painting with fantastic flowers and other natural elements.

    UNESCO source
    05

    Kosiv

    Graphic contour drawing, figurative motifs, and characteristic green and yellow ceramic colours.

    UNESCO source
